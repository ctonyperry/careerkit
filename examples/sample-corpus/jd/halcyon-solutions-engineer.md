# Solutions Engineer

Halcyon Robotics builds fleet-management software for warehouse robots. Our
customers run thousands of robots across dozens of sites, and every one of
them needs to plug our platform into their identity provider, their warehouse
management system, and their reporting stack.

## What you will do

- Own the technical relationship with enterprise customers from the first
  security review through go-live.
- Lead SSO and SCIM rollouts against Okta, Entra ID, and on-prem Active
  Directory.
- Scope and build integrations between the Halcyon Fleet API and customer
  systems, and write the data contracts those integrations follow.
- Be the escalation point when an integration breaks in production: read the
  logs, find the cause, and get engineering the reproduction they need.
- Answer security questionnaires and coordinate evidence requests during
  procurement.
- Help build our enablement program for new solutions engineers.

## What we are looking for

- 5+ years in a customer-facing technical role (solutions engineering,
  implementation, or technical consulting).
- Hands-on experience with SSO (SAML, OIDC) and SCIM provisioning.
- Experience debugging integrations from logs, and explaining what you found
  to both engineers and customers.
- Experience writing data contracts or API specifications with customers.
- Comfort answering enterprise security questionnaires.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent experience.

## Nice to have

- Experience running or contributing to a technical enablement program.
- Experience with Kubernetes or cloud infrastructure operations.
- Familiarity with ROS 2.

Portland, OR (hybrid) or remote in the Pacific time zone. Travel up to 25%.
